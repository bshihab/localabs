HOW IT WORKS

Scan. Read. Ask.

01

Scan your report

Open Apple's native document scanner and capture one or more pages of your printed lab report. Edge detection and perspective correction are built in — Apple Vision OCR pulls every test name, value, and reference range straight from the image.

02

Get plain-language translation

The on-device AI rewrites your report into five sections: a patient summary, questions for your doctor, targeted dietary advice, a medical glossary, and notes on any medications mentioned. Each report gets an AI-generated title for your history.

03

Ask follow-up questions

Highlight any line from the original scan to chat about it. The conversation sees your full profile, recent Apple Health data, and the rest of the report — so the answers are personalized to you, not a generic web search.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Your lab results never leave your phone.

There's no cloud, no account, no analytics, no telemetry. Localabs ships with a quantized medical-tuned model that runs on your iPhone's Neural Engine and GPU. Apple Health data stays on-device. Every conversation is local.

  • No login or email required, ever
  • The AI model is downloaded once (~2.5 GB) and runs offline
  • HealthKit data is read locally — never transmitted
  • Open quantized model: unsloth/medgemma-4b-it-GGUF
  • No third-party SDKs, no telemetry beacons

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common questions.

Does Localabs replace my doctor?

No. Localabs translates lab reports into language you can understand and helps you prepare for the conversation with your clinician. Every report ends with explicit questions to bring up at your next visit.

What lab reports does it work with?

Any printed lab report with test names, your values, and reference ranges — common panels like CBC, comprehensive metabolic, lipid, thyroid, hemoglobin A1c, and vitamin D all work. Multi-page reports are supported via the document scanner.

Why on-device?

Lab data is among the most sensitive personal data you have. Running the AI on your phone means no third party — not even us — ever sees your results.

What model does it use?

By default, Google's MedGemma 4B (a medical-tuned Gemma) quantized to ~2.5 GB. There's also a smaller TinyLlama 1.1B fallback for older devices or for testing.

How much storage does it use?

The default model is ~2.5 GB, downloaded once. Scanned reports add a few hundred KB each. Everything else (chat state, settings, profile) is tiny.

Does it require an Apple Watch?

No. Phone-only users get steps, walking distance, walking speed, and any vitals they've logged manually. A paired Watch adds resting HR, HRV, VO₂ max, sleep, and other passive measurements.

Will my profile data ever leave my phone?

No. Profile (age, conditions, medications, family history, etc.) lives in your device's UserDefaults and is read locally every time a chat or analysis runs.
